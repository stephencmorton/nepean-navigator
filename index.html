    How to read your room numbers

      Add to your Home Screen for quick access, even without internet.

      iPhone / iPad: In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Enable Open as Web App if offered, then tap Add.

      Android: In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu, then tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

      Open the app online once to download it for offline use.

      Room numbering explained

      A four-digit room number gives the floor, building, then the room. For example, 2410 means floor 2, building 4, room 10.
